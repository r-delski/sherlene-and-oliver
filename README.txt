Tips
- Use .jpg files. Keep each one under about 500 KB so the page opens quickly on phones.
- Photos are cropped to fit their frame, so keep faces near the centre.
- Until a photo is added, its frame shows the sample drawing from the "sample" folder.
  As soon as you save your own .jpg here with the right name, it replaces the sample.
